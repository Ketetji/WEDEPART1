Frequently Asked Questions

How often should my solar panels be cleaned?

For standard residential areas, bi-annual cleaning (every 6 months) maintains peak efficiency. Systems near busy roads, construction sites, or farmlands benefit from quarterly cleans due to heavier dust accumulation.

Does rainfall clean solar panels naturally?

Rain removes light surface dust, but it acts like rain on a car windshield—leaving mud streaks, mineral spots, and soot film behind. Specialized pure-water scrubbing is required to lift stubborn film layers.

Will cleaning scratch my panel glass or void warranty?

No. We use ultra-soft nylon bristles engineered specifically for solar glass paired with non-pressurized pure water. We never use chemicals or harsh abrasive pads that invalidate manufacturer warranties.
